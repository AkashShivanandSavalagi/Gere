How to Play Gere

1. Each player has 3 pawns on a 9-node board.

2. Placement: Alternate placing 1 pawn per turn onto any empty node.

3. Movement: Select your pawn and move it along a connected line to an adjacent empty node. No jumping.

4. Win: Align 3 of your pawns in a straight horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line. No move limit — matches end on a win, clock expiry, or the 21-minute hard deadline.
